## What this says about the double-count bug

The 2025 code we recovered credited a nominee in several categories with the votes from all of them. The categories copied word for word above add up to 100% (±0.02), including the ones with nominees that also appear elsewhere: Asmongold and Hasan Piker (Antagonist, Brainlet), Legal Mindset (Brainlet, Neurodivergent), Lauren DeLaguna (Antagonist, Neurodivergent). If those percentages were the site's own output, that suggests the live site didn't double-count, or computed percentages in a way that hid it.

**Omegalul adds up to 125.02% on the page itself** (checked in the page source). Its winner, "Destiny Makes a Joke", has 45.98%, exactly the Godstiny winner's figure, and "Destiny Uses his Premonition Powers" is in both categories. Two readings:
- **A copy slip on the wiki (more likely).** Someone pasted Godstiny's 45.98% into the Omegalul winner's row. Without it the other six add to 79.04%, so the real figure would be 20.96%, and "Destiny Makes a Joke" would still win (next is 18.64%).
- **The double-count bug.** If the site had credited Premonition Powers with its Godstiny votes as well, it would be the nominee with the inflated share. It has 14.38%, which doesn't fit.

Worth asking RiN_LuX for the real Omegalul numbers. Every other category copied word for word adds to 100%.
